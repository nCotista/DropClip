## Problem and specification

`MobileNode` always declared Tier 2 and `clip.push`, including on iOS. Both PIN pairing and QR pairing persisted Tier 2 for iOS peers. SRS §1.3.2 defines iOS as Tier 3, and Protocol capability table forbids Tier 3 from advertising `clip.push`. Read workspace rules and relevant pairing/tier specifications before changing code.

## Change

Derive declared tier from the identity OS: iOS 3, Android 2. Use it in `hello` and both pairing calls. Advertise `clip.text` on iOS; Android retains `clip.text` and `clip.push`. Scope is declared metadata; background lifecycle and manual receive policy are separate acceptance work. No crypto, certificate contract, PIN or transport implementation was changed.

**Diff: 99 lines**, 2 code/test files only; progress/worklogs remain solely on the tracker branch.
