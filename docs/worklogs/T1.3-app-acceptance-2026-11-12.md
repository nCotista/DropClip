## Change

The original network tests established pairing and mTLS but did not inspect the production client's request field sets. Commit `ffae323` adds assertions at the real `TlsTransportServer` pairing boundary and delegates to the real `PairingHost` handler. PIN and QR successes plus wrong-PIN rejection must use the exact request fields for `/pair`, `/confirm`, `/finalize`; the OOB PIN cannot be a direct request value, and responses cannot contain PIN/PIN-hash/PIN-nonce/SAS fields. Payloads stay in memory and are not logged. The existing `cert` field is observed without changing its contract or deciding LEAD's outstanding review question.
