## Root cause and scope

Desktop creates DevicesPage once in main.dart with an early snapshot of mesh.connected. The peerChanges stream is broadcast and does not replay events sent while the page is absent. Opening Devices after a connection, or returning after a hidden disconnect/reconnect, therefore starts from that old snapshot and can remain wrong until the next event. This reproduces the previously observed Offline label despite a ready connection.

Replace the internal connected-set argument with a getter. Read the current authenticated mesh state when StreamBuilder mounts, then receive live events normally. Current connection status remains independent of mDNS disappearance (Protocol §6.4 / SRS FR-10). The change preserves layout, translations, trust/transport rules and heartbeat behavior. No polling or additional persistent subscription is introduced.
