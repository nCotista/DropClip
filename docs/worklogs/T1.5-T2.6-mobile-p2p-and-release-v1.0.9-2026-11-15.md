## 2. Technical Context & Key Decisions (การตัดสินใจเชิงเทคนิค)
* **Core P2P Pairing & Grace Fallback (`PR #116`, `4b1b429`, `998503d`, 249 diff lines):** Extended `address_selector.dart`, `PairingClient` (`port` field in `/api/v1/pair`), `PairingEngine`/`PairingHost` (`clientIp`/`clientPort` in `PairingCompleted`), and `PeerMesh` same-class initiator tie-breaker + grace fallback.
* **Mobile P2P Node & Bidirectional Sync (`PR #117`, `f702204`, `15c79cb`, 384 diff lines):** Updated `MobileNode` & `MobilePeer` to host pairing (`openPairing`, `confirmPairing`, `pairingEvents`), store host/client addresses in `hosts.json`, and exchange clipboard updates and streamed files both ways between phones. Fixed deterministic smaller-ID test selection (`e1afec1`) and `close_sinks` analyzer lint (`15c79cb`).
* **Mobile P2P UI & Camera Permission (`PR #118`, `b206bcd`, `098e641`, 339 diff lines):** Rebased onto `main` and retargeted base to `main`; added Host/Join pairing sheet (QR display, PIN display, SAS confirmation), OS device icons, TH/EN strings (`mobile_strings.dart`), and iOS `NSCameraUsageDescription` (`apps/mobile/ios/Runner/Info.plist`). Fixed async `PairingSasReady` frame settling in Host widget tests (`098e641`).
* **Release `v1.0.9` & Test Port Isolation (`PR #119`, `95fcd7b`, `586ec8e`, 55 diff lines):** Bumped `apps/desktop/pubspec.yaml` & `apps/mobile/pubspec.yaml` (`1.0.9+10`) and `appVersion` (`'1.0.9'`) in `desktop_node.dart` & `mobile_node.dart` (`cf24e32`). Diagnosed `PIN pair persists peer port...` failures on `ubuntu-latest`/`windows-latest`: `melos run test` runs `dropclip_desktop` and `dropclip_mobile` concurrently while `startPhone` bound shared `53317..53417`, causing restarted responders to shift ports away from the saved port in `hosts.json`. Polled `restarted.connected` (`25c1ace`), added optional `firstPort`/`lastPort` parameters to `MobileNode.start` (defaulting to `kTransportPortFirst..kTransportPortLast`), and isolated `startPhone` in `mobile_node_test.dart` onto ephemeral ports while rebinding `responderPort` on restart (`586ec8e`).
